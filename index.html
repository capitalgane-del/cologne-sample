FAQ

What's a decant?

A decant is fragrance transferred from a full retail bottle into a smaller spray bottle. You get the real scent for a fraction of the price of a full bottle.

What's a dupe?

A dupe is a fragrance from a different brand made to smell like a famous, more expensive one. Every dupe here says what it's inspired by, with Parfumo links to both so you can read reviews and compare before you buy.

Are they authentic?

Yes. Every decant is poured from a genuine bottle of the brand on the label. Dupes are the real Lattafa, Armaf, French Avenue and so on. They're not the original designer scent, and we always label them clearly.

Do I pay online?

No. Placing an order reserves it for you. You pay cash or bank transfer when you collect.

How long do they last?

A 5ml decant is roughly 70 sprays, and a 10ml is roughly 140. That's weeks to months of daily wear.
